I've always found that phases in life brings forward a cherished piece of music. Those I hold dear to time come mostly from [Richard Wagner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Wagner). In no specific order those are:

From the beginning of my travels [Die Meistersinger von Nürnberg Overture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9-JaIa8fBKA) sparked that piece of exploration and innocence that I felt venturing out. [Das Rheingold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BILqwUUUpQ) was played when I was in places I felt extreme beauty and achievement such as the Grand Canyon and Everest. [Lohengrin, Act III Prelude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IegneIVbNms) in the case the pandemonium that took place in January 2025. [Lohengrin Prelude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyodILZEQFg) & [ Parsifal Prelude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fla6dyc8ZnA) will always stay in my heart for the times she has provided me comfort in huge distress or in need of emotional warmth, I feel like a baby cradled when I listen to them.
