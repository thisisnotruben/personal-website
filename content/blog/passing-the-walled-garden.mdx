Big tech has corralled the internet to a few websites and the amount of data pulled from you is quite insane, no new thoughts there.

I've been having a new endeavor of self-hosting my “carved out piece of the internet” which some is public and the other is private.

This very website is hosted on my laptop and the images presented here come from that external hard drive shown.

It's a bit more than ownership of digital stuff, it's that plus the expression that I'm not confined to this “walled garden” that big tech has placed: “Instagram, Tiktok, Apple, etc..”.

Knowledge is everywhere on how to do this, and I'll be going ahead and add to that pile on how this is done. For my own sake of record keeping, and for the benefit of others who do a random search of "how is ‘such and such done’". Many small blogs helped out my in process, so I would like to give back.

What the future of this blog will be filled with:

* What kind of apps can be hosted and probably what shouldn't
* How to run said apps in a containerized fashion for portability and ease of configuration.
* Network administration: local network DNS-resolution/firewalls and how the public internet can communicate with your services/apps. Ensuring HTTPS is secure with TLS certificates with reverse-proxy servers.
* Performance gains made with caching responses server side at the proxy for faster delivery.
* Backup up media and services with encryption to another local drive or an object-storage hosted somewhere else.
* Learning how to make your own VPN server for private connections, and/or reverse-proxy with cloudflare for public endpoints.
